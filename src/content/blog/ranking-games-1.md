As my first blog post for the website, I'm going to rank every single one of my games. Y'all ready?

___

## S Tier

- ### [Battle Orbs](https://orbiverse.itch.io/battle-orbs)
*This* game is awesome. Funnily enough, it's one of my worst performing games according to its analytics. But, it's ok. Go ahead and play it! It's a roguelike arena shooter with super simple *Orb* graphics.

- ### [Idle Orbs: 2 Remastered](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orb-2-remastered)
I *love* this game. In general, I already really loved the original [Idle Orbs: 2](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs-2), and I feel like I really did it justice in the remaster!

___

## A Tier

- ### [Idle Orbs: 4](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs-4)
I kinda wish I could go back and do it a little differently. But, I still think, for what it is, it's pretty awesome and I still play it to this day.

- ### [Idle Orbs: 3](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs-3)
Similar to Idle Orbs: 4, I still play it and think they're both worth the $3.

___

## B Tier

- ### [Idle Orbs: 2](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs-2)
Pretty fun, but not well balanced. I was still in high school when I made this and it was my magnum opus at the time. I still think back on it very fondly.

- ### [Idle Orbs: Remastered](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs-remastered)
This was my first remastered game, and I did it out of spite. I HATED the original.

- ### [Idle Orbs: Merge Defense](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs-tower-merge)
I made this game in a quick few days. I really like it! Maybe I should go back in and update it, add more features, etc.

___

## C Tier
- ### [Idle Orbs: Tycoon](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs-tycoon)
Not too much content. But, for what it is, it's pretty awesome. I can't knock it down too much since it *is* just a tech demo of me testing a game engine I was working on. 

- ### [Click and Rage 2: Hue & Saturation](https://orbiverse.itch.io/click-and-rage-2)
A LOT better than the original. Personally, I don't particularly enjoy "rage games" like this, but it was a lot of fun to make.

___

## D Tier

- ### [Idle Orbs](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs)
The OG! My first attempt at moving visuals in a game. Unfortunately, it was the best I could do at the time and I quickly improved after publishing it. So, realizing how bad it actually was, I kinda resented it.

- ### [Idle Orbs: Bees](https://orbiverse.itch.io/idle-orbs-bees)
I made this game in a couple days because I hadn't made a game in too long and was bored. It's a pretty cool idea, but not very balanced.

- ### [Wizardry and Ichor](https://orbiverse.itch.io/wizardry-and-ichor)
This was my first time using "Svelte" to make my web games. Very cool! Unfortunately the game itself isn't much to talk about.

- ### [Click and Rage](https://orbiverse.itch.io/click-and-rage)
This was my first time using Godot. I just used a free asset pack. Not much effort behind it, but still not entirely bad if you like rage games.
